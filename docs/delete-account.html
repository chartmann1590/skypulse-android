SkyPulse · Privacy policy

Delete your SkyPulse account

You can do this in your browser. You do not need to install the Android app.

What gets deleted

Your SkyPulse sign-in, synced aircraft, airports and areas, and public flight links associated with your account. This cannot be undone.

This does not delete your Google account, data stored only on your devices, older guest and unattributed share links, or feedback that you chose to post publicly on GitHub. Clear the SkyPulse app or browser storage separately if you want to remove local data.

SkyPulse keeps a restricted record of the former account ID and deletion time to prevent old sessions from restoring deleted data. It contains no email address or saved items.
