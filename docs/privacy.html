Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Short version: Start tracking flights without signing in. If you choose to sign in, SkyPulse syncs certain saved items to your account. Sharing a flight or submitting feedback is optional and may make the information you share public.

Accounts and saved data

You can use basic flight tracking without an account. If you choose email or Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication processes account information such as your email address and account ID. When you are signed in, saved aircraft, airports and areas (including area labels and coordinates) are synced to Cloud Firestore under your account. Local alert rules and cached flight data are not part of that account sync.

Location and live data

If you grant location permission, SkyPulse uses your device location to center the map, find nearby aircraft, and check location-based alerts. A nearby-aircraft request from Android can send your precise device coordinates to ADS-B.lol or the surrounding search area to OpenSky. SkyPulse Web can send your device coordinates to Airplanes.live when finding nearby aircraft. Map providers receive tile requests for the visible area. These services also receive network information such as your IP address. You can deny location permission and still view aircraft around a default or selected area.

Public flight sharing

If you share a flight, the app stores a snapshot of the aircraft and related flight information in Cloud Firestore and creates a public link. It is not a live feed. The account association for new links is stored separately and privately. Some older links include a Firebase account or anonymous app ID; legacy web links without an account reference cannot be matched to an account.

Delete your account and data

Delete your SkyPulse account in any browser, or open this page from Profile in the Android app. Deletion removes your SkyPulse sign-in, synced saved aircraft, airports and areas, and attributable public share links. A restricted record of the former account ID and deletion time prevents old sessions from recreating that data.

Public feedback

If you post a report or comment from the Android app, the content passes through a Cloudflare-hosted service to GitHub. Screenshots or system diagnostics you choose to attach can also appear publicly on GitHub. Do not include private information.

Diagnostics and app services

Firebase services support app analytics, crash reports, performance measurements, configuration and push notifications. These services can process app usage, device and app identifiers, diagnostic information and push registration data. See Firebase privacy information.

Website analytics

The SkyPulse website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and usage. Google may process page visits, browser or device information, identifiers, and approximate location derived from IP. See how Google uses information from sites and apps.

Ads

AdMob serves banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-based location, ad interactions and technical data to serve and measure ads. The app uses Google’s consent form where required. SkyPulse rewards and the ad-free timer are stored on your device. Read more about how Google uses information from apps.

Limits of free ADS-B data

Not every aircraft is guaranteed to appear. Coverage varies by area and time, data can be delayed or missing, and some aircraft may be hidden or incomplete. Free feeds may not provide reliable gate, boarding or route status.

Third-party open data services

  • ADSB.lol — primary live aircraft data
  • OpenSky Network — fallback live aircraft data
  • adsbdb — flight route lookup (scheduled origin/destination by callsign; used for flight details and the airport arriving/departing board)
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles

Airport and airline reference data from OpenFlights is bundled in the app.

Children

SkyPulse is a general-audience aviation utility and is not directed to children.

Contact

Use the SkyPulse GitHub project for general support. GitHub issues are public: do not post passwords, account information or personal data there.
